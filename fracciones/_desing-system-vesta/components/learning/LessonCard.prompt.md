The unit of the catalogue and the dashboard: one lesson, one card.

```jsx
<LessonCard subject="physics" title="Caída libre" summary="Por qué una pluma y un martillo caen igual en el vacío."
  meta={<><span>12 min</span><span>8 ejercicios</span></>} progress={40} />
```

The discipline eyebrow is the only all-caps text in the system. Summaries are one sentence and never begin with "Aprende a…".
